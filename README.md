# The World Today — Thursday, 19 November 2026

A one-image newspaper front page summing up the world news of Thursday
19 November 2026. A neon-sunset lead panel marks the global launch of GTA VI
after a 13-year wait, alongside panels on Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO,
Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth, Hakeem Jeffries becoming the
first African-American Speaker of the US House, the EU's proposed €45bn loan
for Ukraine, the tanker struck in the Strait of Hormuz, North Korea's
ballistic-missile launches, and the Pew projection that Islam will become the
world's largest religion by 2035 — plus an "Also Today" strip covering
Prague, Spain, Estonia and Kenya. The poster is drawn entirely from scratch
with Python and Pillow (`render.py`); `main.py` delivers the finished
`entry.png`.
